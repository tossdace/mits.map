About the Institute

Muthoot Institute of Technology & Science

MITS is a self-financing technical institution offering undergraduate and postgraduate engineering programmes. The campus is in the Kochi industrial suburb, near Smart City and InfoPark.

It is approximately 15 km from Vyttila Junction toward Muvattupuzha on the Cochin–Madurai National Highway.

Artificial Intelligence & Data Science

Led by Dr. Cerene Mariam Abraham, the department focuses on AI, machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing and data analytics.

  • B.Tech in CSE (Artificial Intelligence) — 120 seats
  • B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science — 60 seats
  • M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science — 12 seats
