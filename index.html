About

Muthoot Institute of Technology & Science

MITS is a self-financing technical institution offering undergraduate and postgraduate engineering programmes. The campus is in the Kochi industrial suburb, near Smart City and InfoPark.

Approximately 15 km from Vyttila Junction toward Muvattupuzha on the Cochin–Madurai National Highway.
